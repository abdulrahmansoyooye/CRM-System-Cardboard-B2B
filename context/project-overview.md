# Cardboard Box Manufacturing Company Website

## Project Overview

This project is a production-grade corporate website and business management platform for a cardboard box manufacturing company. It serves as the company's primary digital presence, showcasing its products, manufacturing capabilities, infrastructure, certifications, industries served, and corporate information while generating qualified sales leads through inquiry and quotation requests.

The platform consists of two major systems:

1. **Public Website** — A fast, responsive, SEO-optimized corporate website designed for customers, distributors, procurement teams, and business partners.
2. **Administrative Management System** — A secure dashboard that enables authorized staff to manage products, website content, blogs, events, careers, inquiries, quotations, testimonials, and company settings.

The application is being **refactored into a production-ready system**, not developed from scratch. The primary objective is to improve architecture, maintainability, scalability, security, accessibility, performance, and overall engineering quality while preserving all existing business functionality.

# Core User Flows

## Public Website Flow

1. Visitor lands on the homepage.
2. Visitor learns about the company.
3. Visitor explores products.
4. Visitor reviews industries served.
5. Visitor views manufacturing capabilities.
6. Visitor checks certifications and infrastructure.
7. Visitor reads blogs or company updates.
8. Visitor submits an inquiry or requests a quotation.
9. Company receives the lead in the admin dashboard.
10. Sales team follows up with the customer.

---

## Product Discovery Flow

1. Visitor opens the Products page.
2. Filters products by category.
3. Opens a product detail page.
4. Reviews specifications and customization options.
5. Requests a quotation.

---

## Career Flow

1. Candidate visits Careers.
2. Reviews open positions.
3. Opens a job description.
4. Uploads a resume.
5. Application is stored for HR review.

---

## Content Management Flow

1. Administrator logs in.
2. Opens dashboard.
3. Creates or edits website content.
4. Saves changes.
5. Updated content becomes available on the public website.
